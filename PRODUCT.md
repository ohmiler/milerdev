# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MilerDev serves Thai-speaking learners who want to learn coding through online courses, from beginners and students to people preparing for real project work. It also serves instructors and admins who manage courses, lessons, bundles, payments, certificates, announcements, blog content, media, and platform operations.

Learners usually arrive with a practical goal: choose a course, understand what they will be able to build, pay or enroll safely, continue lessons, track progress, and return later without friction. Admin users need dense, reliable tools that make repeated operational work easy to scan and hard to misuse.

## Product Purpose

MilerDev is a Thai-language LMS and course commerce platform for teaching web development and programming. Success means learners can quickly understand the learning path, trust the platform, enroll with confidence, keep learning, and see clear progress. For admins, success means course, payment, enrollment, certificate, and content workflows stay fast, readable, secure, and recoverable.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, technical, and friendly. The interface should feel like a bright coding studio: focused, trustworthy, hands-on, and developer-native without becoming cold or overly corporate.

The voice should be direct Thai copy with useful verbs and concrete outcomes. It should avoid inflated marketing claims and should help users decide what to do next.

## Anti-references

Do not make MilerDev feel like a generic SaaS template or a cluttered course marketplace. Avoid nested cards, decorative marketing-card clutter, purple-blue default gradients, vague buzzword copy, washed-out gray text, oversized hero noise, and visuals that hide the actual product or course state.

Admin and dashboard screens should not feel like landing pages. They should be operational, dense, scannable, and calm. Learning screens should not compete with the lesson content.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the learning job: every public page should make the course outcome, next step, and learning path obvious.
2. Preserve focus in learning flows: video, lesson content, progress, and next action matter more than decoration.
3. Make commerce trustworthy: prices, payment states, enrollment access, and errors must be clear and conservative.
4. Keep operations scannable: admin and dashboard surfaces should prioritize tables, filters, status, and fast repeated actions.
5. Respect Thai reading comfort: copy needs generous line-height, clear hierarchy, and no cramped buttons or overflowing labels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-visible focus states on every interactive element. Thai body copy should generally use 1.7 to 1.8 line-height. Icon-only controls need accessible labels. Motion should respect reduced-motion preferences, especially in dashboard, admin, and learning surfaces. Error and empty states should explain the next recovery action without exposing sensitive implementation details.
