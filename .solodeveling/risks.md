# Risks

- Pre-existing deleted and untracked files must not be restored, removed, or overwritten unintentionally.
- Homepage course and bundle claims must come from real data; inferred marketing claims can damage learner trust.
- Dynamic announcements and affiliate banners can introduce layout shift and motion/accessibility issues.
- Browser-based visual verification depends on a usable local browser session and representative local data.
